## Illustrative data check

- Home sample testimonials are explicitly marked “Sample reviews · not real endorsements” and “Illustrative” on each card. They are excluded from the real rating aggregate (`components/dashboard/sample-reviews.tsx`, `app/page.tsx`).
- Example recipe cards and detail pages are labeled “Curated example · Untested” / “Example recipe,” and the home catalog-count banner says examples are excluded (`components/skill-card.tsx`, `app/skills/[slug]/page.tsx`, `app/page.tsx`).
- Dashboard has a prominent illustrative banner, and chart controls say sample, but the **four KPI totals are not individually labeled**. Sample creator/recipe rankings and event totals need consistent adjacent labels. The separate “Live catalog snapshot” uses real approved listings. Thus the requirement “all illustrative data is labeled alongside totals” is **not met**.
- Star ratings from real recipe/account reviews are database-backed. Account identity is not independently verified; a recorded download is an account activity signal, not proof of use. No installation, adapter execution, payment, savings, or outcome measurement is live.

## Priority recommendations for counsel and product owner

1. Replace “Verified Safe” everywhere with language about findings in static checks. Explain clearly that scans are not antivirus or a runtime sandbox, and admin approval does not certify safety or quality.
2. Correct the Privacy retention statement after verifying the 12-month cleanup job in each environment. Counsel should finalize retention, operator and jurisdiction wording; this audit does not edit legal pages.
3. Put “Sample” beside every dashboard KPI total and sample ranking/event count. Keep sample reviews excluded from real aggregate ratings.
4. Change unmeasured “reduces risk,” “people can trust,” and automatic agent-loading phrases to narrow descriptions of checks, files and user-controlled installation.
5. Clarify that download counts are unique signed-in account server requests per recipe version, not installations or completed runs; ratings are from account holders whose real-world identities are not independently verified.
6. Correct the changelog claim about removed illustrative dashboard activity and avoid presenting the curated workflow example as a live catalog metric.
7. Rename “Safest” sorting to its real basis, the static scan tier, and “Popular” to its download-request basis. Replace the unmeasured “reduce risk” statement in the shared footer, legacy upload form and scanner demo.
